Modular Movement

A skill-showcase project: an animated 3D character who can run, sprint, jump and crouch, with a small environment to move around in.

  1. Character animations for idle, run, sprint, jump and crouch, with transitions between states.
  2. C# movement mechanics: walking, sprinting, jumping and crouching driven by player input.
  3. A small 3D test environment to run around in and try the movement.
  4. Modular design, so movement abilities can be added or tuned independently.

Burnout Rush

An arcade racing game that placed 2nd in the 3rd-year B.Tech project evaluation at SAITM Gurugram.

  1. Car physics with wheel colliders; drifting with skid marks, tyre smoke and sound.
  2. AI opponents on waypoints (Easy / Normal / Hard); modes Classic, Escape, Survival, Infection and Special.
  3. Quick Race for 1 player, or 2 players in split-screen on one keyboard.
  4. 5 tracks (Rural, Arctic, Desert, Urban, Moon); 1–10 laps with checkpoints, lap times, position, speedometer and leaderboard.
  5. LeanTween menus, music player, engine sound that follows speed, switchable cameras.

Controls: Player 1 arrow keys, Player 2 WASD, Enter to select, Esc to go back.

Stack
Unity 2020.3, C#, LeanTween

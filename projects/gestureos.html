GestureOS

Hand-gesture control in the browser with MediaPipe Hands, plus an optional local bridge for the real mouse and keyboard.

In use

Me trying it on my laptop webcam.

Default

Each mode as it opens, before the camera starts.

  1. Hand tracking runs on-device in the browser; no video leaves the machine.
  2. Seven modes: Air Mouse, Air Draw, Slides, Sign Letters (ASL subset), OS Control, offline Voice commands and a k-NN gesture trainer.
  3. Optional Python websocket bridge (pynput or pyautogui) moves the real cursor, clicks, drags, scrolls and presses keys.
  4. Starts paused, with a kill switch (Esc or a held fist) and a watchdog that releases input after 1.5 s without a ping.
  5. One Euro cursor smoothing; installable PWA that works offline.

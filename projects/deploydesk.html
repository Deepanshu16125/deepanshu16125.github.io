DeployDesk

An on-prem help desk that answers from a company’s own docs, cites where each sentence came from, and hands the question to a person when it doesn’t know.

Ask

Questions stream in with numbered citations. Clicking a number opens the passage it came from.

Conversations

Every question asked, with its rating and whether it became a ticket.

Tickets

When the bot can't help, the question goes to a person with the answer and sources attached.

Knowledge base

Docs are parsed, chunked and embedded on the server. Nothing goes to an outside API.

Analytics

Live numbers from the server, plus the offline eval results.

Settings

Per-customer config, saved back to the YAML file.

Eval results

Not a UI view: the saved outputs of the Docker and eval runs, shown as a terminal.

  1. Answers questions from a company's own docs (PDF, Markdown, HTML) and cites the passage behind every sentence. The demo knowledge base is 67 public docs split into 1,409 passages, for a made-up hosting company called Kestrel Cloud.
  2. Hybrid search (FAISS vectors + BM25, merged with reciprocal rank fusion) finds the right passage in the top 5 for 0.92 of my 63 labelled questions, against 0.86 for vector search alone.
  3. Runs fully on-prem: Qwen2.5-3B on Ollama on CPU, first token in about 1.6 s. A cross-encoder decides when to refuse. It refused 10 of 10 off-topic questions and none of the real ones.
  4. Tightening the prompt (3 passages, temperature 0, one claim per sentence in the source's words) raised the share of cited sentences an NLI check confirms from 43% to 54%, and right-document citations from 68% to 81%. 54% is still the number I most want to push up.
  5. Ships as a 1.81 GB Docker image that is healthy in about 4.5 s, with 30 unit tests and 12 browser tests that click through every view.

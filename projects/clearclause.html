ClearClause

An AI legal contract analyser that turns a PDF contract into plain-English risk insight.

  1. Reads PDFs up to 15 MB with PyMuPDF and PyPDF2.
  2. Returns an executive summary and a 0–100 Fairness Score.
  3. Rates clause risk HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW, with plain-English explanations and negotiation tips.
  4. OpenAI gpt-4o-mini in JSON mode for structured output; runs locally.
